The codec pack lets Phorepoc play H.264, HEVC, VP8 and VP9 video and AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis and FLAC audio in MP4,
Matroska and Ogg files. It's [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) ported to Symbian, built as its own library
(`pheffmpeg.dll`, LGPL 2.1 or later) that the video player and the software video decoder plug-in load at run time.

Some of these formats are covered by patents in some countries, which is why the pack isn't part of any Phorepoc
image. Whether to install it is up to you.
