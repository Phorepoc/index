# Logos

A listing of a ported project shows that project's own logo, unmodified (only scaled and centred on a transparent
square, which the schema's square icon needs). Our own apps, and projects without an official logo, use icons drawn in
the [Etesian design language](https://language.etesian-design.com) by `tools/make_icons.py`. Each logo below keeps its
owner's terms; the listing's `icon_credit` repeats the credit next to the icon on the website.

|---|---|---|---|

(in the site footer) |
| `gtk-symbian-x32` | GTK logo | [gtk.org](https://www.gtk.org/assets/img/logo-gtk-sm.png) | By Andreas Nilsson, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) ([GTK logo page](https://wiki.gnome.org/Projects/GTK/Logo)) |
| `netsurf-symbian-*` | NetSurf icon | The NetSurf source, [`frontends/gtk/res/netsurf.xpm`](https://github.com/netsurf-browser/netsurf/blob/master/frontends/gtk/res/netsurf.xpm) | Part of NetSurf, GPL-2.0, (c) The NetSurf Developers; the website states no separate logo terms |

Not used, and why:

| Project | Why our generic icon stays |
|---|---|
| WebKit | [webkit.org terms](https://webkit.org/terms-of-use/): "WebKit and the WebKit logo are trademarks of Apple Inc."; the permission covers the WebKit *word* mark only, not the logo |
| D-Bus, GLib | No official logo published by the project |
| Tailscale | phetaild is an independent client (it joins a Headscale tailnet); Tailscale publishes no logo terms, and its logo would suggest an affiliation |
